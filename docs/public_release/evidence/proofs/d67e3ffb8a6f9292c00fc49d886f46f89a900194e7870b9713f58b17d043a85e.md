To prove that there exist two real-coefficient polynomials $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ such that $\deg P \ge 2024$, $\deg Q \ge 2$, and $P(Q(x)-x-1)=Q(P(x))$, we utilize the properties of the Chebyshev polynomials of the first kind, $T_n(x)$.

Let $C_n(x) = 2 T_n(x/2)$. These polynomials, known as scaled Chebyshev polynomials, satisfy the composition identity $C_n(C_m(x)) = C_{nm}(x)$. In particular, $C_n(C_2(x)) = C_2(C_n(x))$ for any $n \ge 1$. Note that $C_2(x) = 2 T_2(x/2) = 2(2(x/2)^2 - 1) = x^2 - 2$.

We look for $P$ and $Q$ as shifted versions of $C_n$ and $C_2$. Let $g_s(x) = x+s$ be a translation. We define $Q$ and $f(x) = Q(x)-x-1$ such that they are conjugate to $C_2$ via translations.
Let $Q(x) = g_s^{-1}(C_2(g_s(x))) = (x+s)^2 - 2 - s$.
Then $f(x) = Q(x) - x - 1 = (x+s)^2 - s - 2 - x - 1 = x^2 + (2s-1)x + s^2 - s - 3$.
We want $f(x)$ to be of the form $g_t^{-1}(C_2(g_t(x)))$ for some $t$:
$f(x) = (x+t)^2 - 2 - t = x^2 + 2tx + t^2 - t - 2$.

Comparing the coefficients of $x$:
$2t = 2s - 1 \implies t = s - \frac{1}{2}$.
Comparing the constant terms:
$s^2 - s - 3 = t^2 - t - 2$.
Substituting $t = s - \frac{1}{2}$:
$s^2 - s - 3 = (s - \frac{1}{2})^2 - (s - \frac{1}{2}) - 2$
$s^2 - s - 3 = s^2 - s + \frac{1}{4} - s + \frac{1}{2} - 2 = s^2 - 2s - \frac{5}{4}$.
Solving for $s$:
$s = 3 - \frac{5}{4} = \frac{7}{4}$.
Then $t = \frac{7}{4} - \frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{4}$.

With $s = 7/4$ and $t = 5/4$, we have:
$Q(x) = g_s^{-1} \circ C_2 \circ g_s(x)$ and $f(x) = g_t^{-1} \circ C_2 \circ g_t(x)$.
Now, we define $P(x) = g_s^{-1} \circ C_n \circ g_t(x) = C_n(x + \frac{5}{4}) - \frac{7}{4}$, where $n = 2024$.
We check the condition $P(f(x)) = Q(P(x))$:
LHS: $P(f(x)) = (g_s^{-1} \circ C_n \circ g_t) \circ (g_t^{-1} \circ C_2 \circ g_t) = g_s^{-1} \circ C_n \circ C_2 \circ g_t$.
RHS: $Q(P(x)) = (g_s^{-1} \circ C_2 \circ g_s) \circ (g_s^{-1} \circ C_n \circ g_t) = g_s^{-1} \circ C_2 \circ C_n \circ g_t$.
Since $C_n \circ C_2 = C_{2n} = C_2 \circ C_n$, the equality holds.

Finally, we verify the degree requirements:
$\deg P = n = 2024 \ge 2024$ and $\deg Q = 2 \ge 2$.
Both $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ have real coefficients because $C_n(x)$ and the translations are real.

Thus, the polynomials $P(x) = C_{2024}(x + \frac{5}{4}) - \frac{7}{4}$ and $Q(x) = (x + \frac{7}{4})^2 - \frac{15}{4}$ satisfy the given condition.
