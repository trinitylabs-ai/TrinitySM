To find all non-negative integers $a, b, c$ such that $20^{a}+b^{4}=2024^{c}$, we analyze the equation through modular arithmetic and case analysis.

### 1. Boundary Cases
*   **Case $c=0$:** The equation becomes $20^a + b^4 = 2024^0 = 1$. Since $a, b$ are non-negative integers, the only possibility is $a=0$ and $b=0$. This gives the solution $(0, 0, 0)$.
*   **Case $b=0$:** The equation becomes $20^a = 2024^c$. The prime factorization of $20$ is $2^2 \cdot 5$, and the prime factorization of $2024$ is $2^3 \cdot 11 \cdot 23$. For $20^a = 2024^c$ to hold, the sets of prime factors must be identical. Since $20$ has a factor of 5 and $2024$ does not, we must have $a=0$. Then $1 = 2024^c$, which implies $c=0$. This returns the solution $(0, 0, 0)$.
*   **Case $a=0$:** The equation becomes $1 + b^4 = 2024^c$.
    *   If $c=0$, we have $1 + b^4 = 1$, so $b=0$, giving $(0, 0, 0)$.
    *   If $c>0$, we check modulo 5: $1 + b^4 \equiv 2024^c \equiv (-1)^c \pmod 5$. Since $b^4 \equiv 0$ or $1 \pmod 5$, we have $1 + b^4 \equiv 1$ or $2 \pmod 5$. For this to equal $(-1)^c \pmod 5$, we must have $1 \equiv (-1)^c \pmod 5$, which implies $c$ is even. Let $c=2k$ for $k \in \mathbb{Z}^+$.
    *   Then $b^4 = 2024^{2k} - 1 = (2024^k - 1)(2024^k + 1)$.
    *   The greatest common divisor $\gcd(2024^k - 1, 2024^k + 1) = \gcd(2024^k - 1, 2) = 1$ because $2024^k - 1$ is odd.
    *   Since the product of two coprime integers is a fourth power, each factor must be a fourth power. Let $2024^k - 1 = u^4$ and $2024^k + 1 = v^4$ for some non-negative integers $u, v$.
    *   Subtracting these gives $v^4 - u^4 = 2$. The only fourth powers are $0, 1, 16, \dots$, and no two fourth powers differ by 2. Thus, there are no solutions for $c > 0$.

### 2. General Case: $a, b, c > 0$
Consider the equation modulo 5:
$20^a + b^4 = 2024^c \implies 0 + b^4 \equiv (-1)^c \pmod 5$.
Since $b^4 \equiv 0$ or $1 \pmod 5$, we must have $b^4 \equiv 1 \pmod 5$ and $c$ must be even. Let $c=2k$ for $k \in \mathbb{Z}^+$.
The equation is $20^a = 2024^{2k} - b^4 = (2024^k - b^2)(2024^k + b^2)$.
Let $X = 2024^k - b^2$ and $Y = 2024^k + b^2$. Then $XY = 20^a = 2^{2a} \cdot 5^a$.
The factors must be of the form $X = 2^x 5^y$ and $Y = 2^z 5^w$ with $x+z=2a$ and $y+w=a$.
Summing these gives:
$X + Y = 2^x 5^y + 2^z 5^w = 2 \cdot 2024^k = 2 \cdot (2^3 \cdot 253)^k = 2^{3k+1} \cdot 253^k$.
Assuming $x \le z$, we divide by $2^x$:
$5^y + 2^{z-x} 5^w = 2^{3k+1-x} \cdot 253^k$.

**Subcase A: $z > x$**
Since the LHS is odd unless $y=0$ or $w=0$, and the RHS is $2^{3k+1-x} \cdot 253^k$, we must have $x = 3k+1$ for the RHS to be odd. Then:
$5^y + 2^{z-x} 5^w = 253^k$.
Since $\gcd(5, 253) = 1$, at least one of $y$ or $w$ must be zero.
*   If $y=0$, then $w=a$. The equation is $1 + 2^{z-x} 5^a = 253^k$.
    Modulo 3: $1 + (-1)^{z-x} (-1)^a \equiv 1^k \equiv 1 \pmod 3$, which implies $(-1)^{z-x+a} \equiv 0 \pmod 3$, a contradiction.
*   If $w=0$, then $y=a$. The equation is $5^a + 2^{z-x} = 253^k$.
    Note that $z-x = (x+z) - 2x = 2a - 2x = 2(a-x)$, so $z-x$ is even.
    Modulo 3: $5^a + 2^{z-x} \equiv (-1)^a + 4^{a-x} \equiv (-1)^a + 1 \pmod 3$.
    Also $253^k \equiv 1^k \equiv 1 \pmod 3$.
    Thus $(-1)^a + 1 \equiv 1 \pmod 3 \implies (-1)^a \equiv 0 \pmod 3$, a contradiction.

**Subcase B: $z = x$**
Then $x=z=a$. The sum becomes $2^a (5^y + 5^w) = 2^{3k+1} \cdot 253^k$.
Dividing by $2^a$ gives $5^y + 5^w = 2^{3k+1-a} \cdot 253^k$.
Since $\gcd(5, 253)=1$, at least one of $y$ or $w$ must be 0. Assume $y=0, w=a$.
$1 + 5^a = 2^{3k+1-a} \cdot 253^k$.
For $k \ge 1$, $1+5^a$ must be divisible by $253 = 11 \cdot 23$.
Checking $5^a \pmod{11}$: the powers are $5^0 \equiv 1, 5^1 \equiv 5, 5^2 \equiv 3, 5^3 \equiv 4, 5^4 \equiv 9, 5^5 \equiv 1 \pmod{11}$.
The values of $1+5^a \pmod{11}$ are $\{2, 6, 4, 5, 10\}$. None are $0 \pmod{11}$.
Thus $1+5^a$ is never divisible by 11, so no solutions exist for $k \ge 1$.

The only non-negative integer solution is $(a, b, c) = (0, 0, 0)$.
