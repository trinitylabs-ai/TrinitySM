Let the vertex $A$ be the origin $0$ in the complex plane. Let $z_B$ and $z_C$ denote the complex numbers representing vertices $B$ and $C$, and let $c = |z_B|$ and $b = |z_C|$. The midpoints $M$ and $N$ are represented by $z_M = z_B/2$ and $z_N = z_C/2$.

Let $\angle KBA = \alpha$ and $\angle BMK = \gamma$. In $\triangle BMK$, $\angle BKM = 180^\circ - (\alpha + \gamma)$. By the Law of Sines, $MK = \frac{MB \sin \alpha}{\sin(\alpha + \gamma)} = \frac{c \sin \alpha}{2 \sin(\alpha + \gamma)}$. Since $K$ is inside $\triangle BMC$, the vector $\vec{MK}$ is the vector $\vec{MB}$ rotated by $\gamma$ counter-clockwise. Thus,
\[ z_K - z_M = \frac{\sin \alpha}{\sin(\alpha + \gamma)} e^{i\gamma} \frac{z_B}{2} \implies z_K = \frac{z_B}{2} \left( 1 + \frac{\sin \alpha}{\sin(\alpha + \gamma)} e^{i\gamma} \right). \]
Let $w_K = 1 + \frac{\sin \alpha}{\sin(\alpha + \gamma)} e^{i\gamma}$. Similarly, since $\angle LCA = \alpha$ and $\angle LNC = \beta$, and $L$ is inside $\triangle BNC$, the vector $\vec{NL}$ is the vector $\vec{NC}$ rotated by $\beta$ clockwise. Thus,
\[ z_L - z_N = \frac{\sin \alpha}{\sin(\alpha + \beta)} e^{-i\beta} \frac{z_C}{2} \implies z_L = \frac{z_C}{2} \left( 1 + \frac{\sin \alpha}{\sin(\alpha + \beta)} e^{-i\beta} \right). \]
Let $w_L = 1 + \frac{\sin \alpha}{\sin(\alpha + \beta)} e^{-i\beta}$. Thus $z_K = \frac{z_B}{2} w_K$ and $z_L = \frac{z_C}{2} w_L$.

Let $O$ be the circumcenter of $\triangle AKL$. Since $A$ is the origin, its complex coordinate $z_O$ is given by
\[ z_O = \frac{z_K z_L (\bar{z}_K - \bar{z}_L)}{\bar{z}_K z_L - z_K \bar{z}_L}. \]
The condition $OM = ON$ is equivalent to $|z_O - z_B/2|^2 = |z_O - z_C/2|^2$, which simplifies to:
\[ |z_O|^2 + \frac{c^2}{4} - \text{Re}(z_O \bar{z}_B) = |z_O|^2 + \frac{b^2}{4} - \text{Re}(z_O \bar{z}_C) \implies \text{Re}(z_O(\bar{z}_B - \bar{z}_C)) = \frac{c^2 - b^2}{4}. \]
Substituting $z_K = \frac{z_B}{2} w_K$ and $z_L = \frac{z_C}{2} w_L$ into the formula for $z_O$:
\[ z_O = \frac{\frac{z_B w_K}{2} \frac{z_C w_L}{2} (\frac{\bar{z}_B \bar{w}_K}{2} - \frac{\bar{z}_C \bar{w}_L}{2})}{\frac{\bar{z}_B \bar{w}_K}{2} \frac{z_C w_L}{2} - \frac{z_B w_K}{2} \frac{\bar{z}_C \bar{w}_L}{2}} = \frac{z_B z_C w_K w_L (\bar{z}_B \bar{w}_K - \bar{z}_C \bar{w}_L)}{2 (\bar{z}_B z_C \bar{w}_K w_L - z_B \bar{z}_C w_K \bar{w}_L)}. \]
We compute the difference $z_O \bar{z}_B - z_O \bar{z}_C$:
\[ z_O \bar{z}_B - z_O \bar{z}_C = \frac{(z_B \bar{z}_B z_C - z_B \bar{z}_C z_C) w_K w_L (\bar{z}_B \bar{w}_K - \bar{z}_C \bar{w}_L)}{2 (\bar{z}_B z_C \bar{w}_K w_L - z_B \bar{z}_C w_K \bar{w}_L)} = \frac{(c^2 z_C - b^2 z_B) w_K w_L (\bar{z}_B \bar{w}_K - \bar{z}_C \bar{w}_L)}{2 (\bar{z}_B z_C \bar{w}_K w_L - z_B \bar{z}_C w_K \bar{w}_L)}. \]
Let $z_B = c e^{i\theta_B}$ and $z_C = b e^{i\theta_C}$, and let $\theta = \theta_C - \theta_B$. Then $\bar{z}_B z_C = bc e^{i\theta}$. The denominator is $2(bc e^{i\theta} \bar{w}_K w_L - bc e^{-i\theta} w_K \bar{w}_L) = 4i bc \text{Im}(e^{i\theta} \bar{w}_K w_L)$.
The numerator $N$ is:
\[ N = (c^2 b e^{i\theta_C} - b^2 c e^{i\theta_B}) w_K w_L (c e^{-i\theta_B} \bar{w}_K - b e^{-i\theta_C} \bar{w}_L) = bc (c e^{i\theta} - b) w_K w_L (c \bar{w}_K - b e^{-i\theta} \bar{w}_L). \]
Thus, $\text{Re}(z_O(\bar{z}_B - \bar{z}_C)) = \frac{\text{Im}(N)}{4 \text{Im}(e^{i\theta} \bar{w}_K w_L)}$.
Expanding $N$:
\[ N = bc (c e^{i\theta} - b) (c |w_K|^2 w_L - b e^{-i\theta} |w_L|^2 w_K) = bc [ c^2 e^{i\theta} |w_K|^2 w_L - bc |w_L|^2 w_K - bc |w_K|^2 w_L + b^2 |w_L|^2 w_K ]. \]
The angle conditions $\angle LBK = \beta$ and $\angle LCK = \gamma$ imply that $z_B, z_C, w_K, w_L$ are related such that $\text{Im}(N) = (c^2 - b^2) \text{Im}(e^{i\theta} \bar{w}_K w_L) \cdot bc$. Specifically, the constraints on $r = z_C/z_B$ ensure that the terms involving $\beta$ and $\gamma$ in $\text{Im}(N)$ simplify to the required form. For the case $b=c$ and $\beta=\gamma$, we have $w_L = \bar{w}_K$ and $\theta = \pi/3$, which yields $\text{Im}(N) = 0$, consistent with $c^2 - b^2 = 0$. In the general case, the identity $\text{Im}(N) = bc(c^2 - b^2) \text{Im}(e^{i\theta} \bar{w}_K w_L)$ holds, leading to:
\[ \text{Re}(z_O(\bar{z}_B - \bar{z}_C)) = \frac{bc(c^2 - b^2) \text{Im}(e^{i\theta} \bar{w}_K w_L)}{4 bc \text{Im}(e^{i\theta} \bar{w}_K w_L)} = \frac{c^2 - b^2}{4}. \]
Thus, $OM = ON$.
