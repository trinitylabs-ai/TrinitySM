## Decision

Reason: Both proofs correctly identify the answer $\binom{p+2}{2}$ and provide identical, fully verified constructions for tightness. The decisive difference lies in the lower bound justification. Proof A cites the minimization result as a known theorem without derivation, leaving the core obligation externally referenced. Proof B provides a self-contained inductive framework using projective duality and convex hull analysis, explicitly tracking the gain of $p+1$ new red edges and bounding the loss to $p+1$ via the structure of the $p$-level chain. Although B states the geometric lemma about the $p$-level chain without proof, it correctly situates it within a rigorous inductive accounting argument that demonstrates the structural mechanism of the bound. This makes B's justification mathematically stronger and more complete as a written solution.
