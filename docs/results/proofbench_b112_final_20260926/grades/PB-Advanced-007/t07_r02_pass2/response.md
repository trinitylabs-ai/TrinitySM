The construction is valid. Taking any integer \(n\ge 2024\),
\[
P(x)=\left(x+\frac54\right)^n-\frac74,\qquad
Q(x)=x^2+\frac72x+\frac{21}{16},
\]
gives
\[
Q(x)-x-1+\frac54=\left(x+\frac54\right)^2.
\]
Hence
\[
P(Q(x)-x-1)=\left(x+\frac54\right)^{2n}-\frac74.
\]
Writing \(t=(x+\frac54)^n\), we also have
\[
Q(P(x))
=\left(t-\frac74\right)^2+\frac72\left(t-\frac74\right)+\frac{21}{16}
=t^2-\frac74,
\]
which is the same expression. Moreover, \(\deg P=n\ge2024\) and \(\deg Q=2\).

The references to matching the “coefficient of \(x^n\)” and the “constant term” are slightly imprecise because the common \((x+c)^{2n}\) term itself contains such coefficients, but subtracting that common term makes the stated conditions fully valid. Thus the proof is complete.
