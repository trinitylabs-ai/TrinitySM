\[
P(x)=\left(x+\frac54\right)^n-\frac74,\qquad
Q(x)=x^2+\frac72x+\frac{21}{16}.
\]
Then
\[
Q(x)-x-1+\frac54
=x^2+\frac52x+\frac{25}{16}
=\left(x+\frac54\right)^2,
\]
so
\[
P(Q(x)-x-1)=\left(x+\frac54\right)^{2n}-\frac74.
\]
Writing \(y=(x+\frac54)^n\), we also have
\[
Q(P(x))
=\left(y-\frac74\right)^2+\frac72\left(y-\frac74\right)+\frac{21}{16}
=y^2-\frac74
=\left(x+\frac54\right)^{2n}-\frac74.
\]
Thus the identity holds for every real \(x\). Moreover,
\[
\deg P=n\ge 2024,\qquad \deg Q=2.
\]
