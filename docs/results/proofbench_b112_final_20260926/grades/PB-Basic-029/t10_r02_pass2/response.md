The initial characterization
\[
N(X)=|\{k:f_k(X)<0\}|
\]
is correct. However, the rest has major gaps:

- A projective transformation sending \(O\) to infinity does not necessarily send every segment \(OX\) to a ray in one fixed vertical direction; the direction can depend on which side of the new line at infinity \(X\) lies.
- The crucial lower-bound theorem for levels is merely asserted. The case \(n=p+2\) does not prove monotonicity for larger \(n\), and the claim that the \(k\)-level has at least \(k+1\) vertices is not justified.
- The proposed extremal construction is not validly established. Placing additional lines “above” the original vertices increases their values of \(N(X)\), so it may remove red vertices. No explicit placement or proof that all new vertices have more than \(p\) lines above them is supplied.

Thus neither the lower bound nor an optimal construction for general \(n\) is proved. The correct formula and reformulation alone do not meet the stated partial-credit criterion.
