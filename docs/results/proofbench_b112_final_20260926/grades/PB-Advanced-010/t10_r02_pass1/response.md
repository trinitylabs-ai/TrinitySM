The preliminary radical-axis observations are valid. However, the central claim that \(M\in C_1\) for every \(X\in OG\) is unsupported and false.

For example, take
\[
A=(0,0),\quad B=(4,0),\quad C=(1,3),\quad X=O=\left(2,\frac43\right).
\]
Then
\[
P=\left(\frac8{11},\frac{24}{11}\right),\quad
Q=\left(\frac{14}{5},0\right),\quad
K=\left(\frac{16}{11},0\right),\quad
M=\left(\frac52,\frac32\right).
\]
The circle through \(K,P,Q\) has equation
\[
x^2+y^2-\frac{234}{55}x-\frac{158}{55}y+\frac{224}{55}=0.
\]
Substitution of \(M\) gives \(-261/110\ne0\), so \(M\notin C_1\).

Moreover, the asserted formula relating \(\operatorname{Pow}_N(T)\), \(\operatorname{Pow}_N(S)\), and \(\operatorname{Pow}_N(P)\) is not a valid general power identity. The solution neither proves \(B,T,P,C\) concyclic nor establishes the required equality of power ratios, so it does not meet either partial-credit criterion.
