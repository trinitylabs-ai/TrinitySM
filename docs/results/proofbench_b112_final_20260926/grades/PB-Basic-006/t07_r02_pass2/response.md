The contradiction setup and deduction \(c_k\ne0\) are correct. The stated Newton inequality and its limiting consequence are also valid coefficient constraints.

However, these inequalities are never used to prove convergence. The central assertion that hyperbolicity of every partial sum forces the formal power series to converge everywhere is invoked as an unproved, highly specialized “Pólya theorem.” This implication contains essentially the main substance of the problem: it requires, for example, the Vieta/AM–GM bound
\[
n\left|\frac{c_n}{c_0}\right|^{2/n}
 \le \left(\frac{c_1}{c_0}\right)^2-2\frac{c_2}{c_0}.
\]
Without proving such a result, the passage to an entire function—and hence the contradiction with integral nonzero coefficients—is unsupported. This is a major gap, not a minor omission.

The valid Newton inequality constitutes relevant partial progress under the specific guideline, but the submission is not almost complete.
