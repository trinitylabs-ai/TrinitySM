\sum_{j=1}^n\frac1{r_j^2}

\qquad
\left|\frac{c_n}{c_0}\right|^{2/n}
\le \frac1n\sum_{j=1}^n\frac1{r_j^2}.
\]
This is precisely what forces \(|c_n|^{1/n}\to0\). Omitting it leaves the main component of the proof unsupported, not merely a minor gap. The submission also does not establish the Vieta inequality required for the stated partial-credit criterion.
