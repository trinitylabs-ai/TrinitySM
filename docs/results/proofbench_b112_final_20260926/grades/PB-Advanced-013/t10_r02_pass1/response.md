The logarithmic and hyperbolic-tangent reformulation is correct, as are several concavity computations. The \(j=2\) comparison also gives a valid local inequality, which qualifies for partial progress.

However, the crucial claim
\[
h(j,S)\ge \frac12\tanh S\qquad(j>2)
\]
is neither proved nor true. Indeed, using \(\tanh z=z-z^3/3+O(z^5)\),
\[
h(j,S)=\frac S2-\frac{4^j-1}{18j^3}S^3+O(S^5).
\]
For \(j=4\),
\[
h(4,S)-\frac12\tanh S=-\frac7{128}S^3+O(S^5)<0
\]
for sufficiently small \(S>0\). Thus the claimed bound on \(V_{\rm neg}\) fails. Concavity of the two functions and agreement at limiting values does not imply their pointwise comparison.

This is a central, false step rather than a minor omission, so the proof cannot receive almost-full credit. The valid reformulation and local \(j=2\) inequality warrant the specified partial credit.
