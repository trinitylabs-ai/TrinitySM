The logarithmic reformulation and several concavity calculations are correct. In particular, the estimate for \(V_{\mathrm{pos}}\) and the final concavity inequality in \(\gamma\) are meaningful localization progress.

However, the crucial estimate
\[
h(j,S)\ge \frac12\tanh S
\]
is neither proved nor true. For \(j=4\), Taylor expansion at \(S=0\) gives
\[
h(4,S)=\frac S2-\frac{85}{384}S^3+O(S^5),
\]
whereas
\[
\frac12\tanh S=\frac S2-\frac{64}{384}S^3+O(S^5).
\]
Thus \(h(4,S)<\frac12\tanh S\) for sufficiently small positive \(S\). Concavity and agreement at limiting values do not imply the asserted comparison. Consequently, the bound on \(V_{\mathrm{neg}}\), and hence the main proof, fails at a central step. This is a major gap rather than a minor verification error.

The correct transformations and localization/concavity estimates merit the specified partial credit.
