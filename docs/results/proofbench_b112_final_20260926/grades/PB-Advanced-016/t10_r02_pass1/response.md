The Angle Bisector Theorem computations for \(AE\) and \(AF\) are correct. However, the crucial collinearity \(L,U,V\) is not proved:

- From the stated lengths,
  \[
  \frac{AU}{UB}=\frac{b}{a+c-b},\qquad
  \frac{AV}{VC}=\frac{c}{a+b-c},
  \]
  not \(b/a\) and \(c/a\).
- Although \(LI\parallel AL'\), this does not imply that \(L\) and \(L'\) divide \(BC\) in the same ratio. Parallel lines through different points generally meet the transversal \(BC\) at different points.
- Consequently, the Menelaus argument is invalid.

The remainder assumes this unproved collinearity and then merely asserts, without deriving from the vector equations or angle relations, that \(IP\parallel XY\). Thus neither the designated partial-credit result nor the final claim is established.
