The algebraic transformations, recurrence, parity argument, and final identity
\[
3u_{2m}+2w_{2m}+1=6u_m^2
\]
are correct.

However, the essential assertion that **all** positive solutions of
\[
U^2-6w^2=3
\]
are
\[
U+w\sqrt6=(3+\sqrt6)(5+2\sqrt6)^n
\]
is not justified. For generalized Pell equations, multiplication by the fundamental unit need not automatically produce every solution; one must exclude additional solution orbits. Here this can be repaired by a short descent argument, but it is necessary because the conclusion that the relevant index \(n\) is even depends on the completeness of this parametrization. The assertion that the resulting \(x\) is integral is also not explicitly checked, though it is true and not essential when proceeding from a given \(x\).

Thus the solution has a correct core and is readily repairable, but it has a notable missing justification.
