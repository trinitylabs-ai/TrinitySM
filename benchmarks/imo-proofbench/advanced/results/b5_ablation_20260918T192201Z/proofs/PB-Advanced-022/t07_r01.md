1.  **Coordinates of the Triangle:**

    The incenter $I$ of a right triangle is $(r, r)$, where $r = \frac{b+c-a}{2} = \frac{4+3-5}{2} = 1$.

    The magnitudes are $|\vec{IN}| = \sqrt{0.5^2 + (-1)^2} = \sqrt{1.25} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$ and $|\vec{IM}| = \sqrt{(-1)^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{2}$.

3.  **Coordinates of $B'$ and $C'$:**

4.  **Calculation of $\angle B'KC'$:**

    Since $\cos(\theta) = -\cos(180^\circ - \theta)$, we have $\angle NIM = 180^\circ - \angle B'KC'$.
