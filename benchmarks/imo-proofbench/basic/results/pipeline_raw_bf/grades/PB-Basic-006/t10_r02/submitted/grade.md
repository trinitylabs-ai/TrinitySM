However, the crucial assertion \(c_kc_{k-2}>0\) is unjustified and false for general real-rooted polynomials. Newton’s inequalities do not imply such sign consistency; for example, \(x^2+x-1\) has distinct real roots but its constant and quadratic coefficients have opposite signs. “No internal gaps” applies under additional sign hypotheses, such as nonnegative coefficients.

Without \(c_kc_{k-2}>0\), the displayed discriminant inequality may be automatic and cannot be converted into the recurrence for \(a_k=|c_k|\). Thus the decay argument and final contradiction do not follow. Repairing this would require a major new argument, such as the fixed-coefficient Vieta/AM-GM inequality in the reference solution, so the proof is not almost complete.
