The contradiction setup and the application of Newton’s inequalities are valid, and the limiting coefficient inequality is a relevant necessary condition.

However, Step 2 contains a major unjustified assertion. Real-rootedness of every truncation does not automatically establish locally uniform convergence or that the formal series has infinite radius of convergence. Invoking the Laguerre–Pólya class presupposes precisely such convergence. The statement that finite radius would “violate the conditions” is not proved, and the preceding Newton inequality alone does not imply coefficient decay.

The essential missing argument is a quantitative Vieta/AM–GM bound such as
\[
\left(\frac{c_1}{c_0}\right)^2-2\frac{c_2}{c_0}
\ge n\left|\frac{c_n}{c_0}\right|^{2/n},
\]
which rigorously forces \(|c_n|^{1/n}\to0\). Steps 3 and 4 would be correct once entire convergence were established, but that is the central unresolved part rather than a minor gap.

Thus the solution earns partial credit for the valid coefficient inequality and contradiction framework, but is not almost complete.
