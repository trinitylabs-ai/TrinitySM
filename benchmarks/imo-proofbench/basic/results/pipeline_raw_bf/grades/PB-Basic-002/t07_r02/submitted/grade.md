The AM-GM applications themselves are correct, especially
\[
x^2+y^2+z^2+t^2\ge 4\sqrt{P}.
\]

However, the crucial deduction \(P\le16\) is invalid. From
\[
S\ge4P^{1/4}
\]
one obtains \(2S\ge8P^{1/4}\), not \(2S\le8P^{1/4}\). Thus \(P\le2S\) and \(S\ge4P^{1/4}\) cannot be combined as claimed. Indeed, \(x=100\) and \(y=z=t=1\) satisfy \(P\le2S\) while \(P=100>16\).

Consequently, the subsequent inequality \(4\sqrt P\ge P\) is unsupported, so the proof does not establish the result. Nevertheless, the correct AM-GM work meets the stated partial-credit criterion.
