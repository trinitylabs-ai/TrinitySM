However, the boundary is not fully covered. The values \(k(0)\) and \(k(1/2)\) represent only \((0,0,1)\) and \((1/2,1/2,0)\), not a general boundary point \((a,1-a,0)\). Thus the asserted global-minimum conclusion has a genuine gap.

The gap is minor and easily repaired: if \(c=0\), then \(ab\leq 1/4\) and
\[
\sqrt a+\sqrt b\geq 2(ab)^{1/4}\geq 3\sqrt3\,ab,
\]
where the latter follows from
\[
3\sqrt3\,(ab)^{3/4}\leq \frac{3\sqrt3}{2\sqrt2}<2.
\]
The other edges follow symmetrically. Hence the solution is almost correct but not complete as submitted.
