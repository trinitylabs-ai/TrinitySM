In Case 2, however, the assertion

> \(g\) is convex and \(g'(\sqrt3)>0\), hence \(g(p)\le g(\sqrt3)\)

is not valid in general. Convexity and a positive derivative at the right endpoint do not alone compare the two endpoint values.

This is a minor, readily repairable gap: one can compute
\[
g(\sqrt2)=9\sqrt3-12\sqrt2<0,\qquad g(\sqrt3)=0.
\]
A convex function lies below the chord joining its endpoint values, so \(g(p)\le0\) throughout \([\sqrt2,\sqrt3]\). With that addition, the proof and equality characterization are complete.
