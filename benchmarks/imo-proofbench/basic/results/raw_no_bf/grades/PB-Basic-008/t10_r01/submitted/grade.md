However, the crucial one-variable analysis is incomplete and contains a false assertion. For
\[
F(x)=2x+\sqrt{1-2x^2}-3\sqrt3(2x^2-3x^4),

\[
F'(x)=2-\frac{2x}{\sqrt{1-2x^2}}-12\sqrt3x+36\sqrt3x^3.

Besides \(x=1/\sqrt3\), there are additional critical points: for example, \(F'(0)>0\) while \(F'(0.1)<0\), and \(F'(0.65)>0\) while \(F'(x)\to-\infty\) as \(x\to1/\sqrt2\). Thus the claim that \(1/\sqrt3\) is the only critical point is false, and the nonnegativity in Case 2 has not been proved.
