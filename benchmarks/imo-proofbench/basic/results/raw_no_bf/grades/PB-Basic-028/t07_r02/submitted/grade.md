The solution makes a useful equivalent reformulation:

- By symmetry about the angle bisector, \(AX=AY\) and \(XI'=I'Y\), so it suffices to prove \(AX=XI'\).
- Apart from the ordering typo \(\triangle AEF\sim\triangle ABC\), the similarity ratio \(\cos A\) and hence
  \[
  AI'=\frac{r\cos A}{\sin(A/2)}
  \]
  are correctly obtained.
- The condition \(AX=XI'\) is correctly transformed into
  \[
  r_W=\frac{r\cos A}{1+\cos A}.
  \]
- The proposed coordinate equation expressing tangency between \((W)\) and the Euler circle is also a potentially useful route.

However, the decisive step is entirely missing. The submission only verifies the desired value of \(r_W\) for an equilateral triangle. A single example does not prove that this value satisfies the tangency quadratic for an arbitrary triangle. The solution neither substitutes the proposed value into the general quadratic and verifies it using triangle identities, nor solves the quadratic and uses the “closer to \(A\)” condition to select the appropriate root. Thus the final assertion that the formula “holds” is unsupported and constitutes a major gap, not a minor omission.

The work nevertheless earns the specified partial credit for obtaining meaningful equivalent information without inversion.
