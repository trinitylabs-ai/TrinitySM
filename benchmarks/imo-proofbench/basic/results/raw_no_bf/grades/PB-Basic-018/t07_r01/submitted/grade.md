The algebraic transformation to \(3v^2-2u^2=1\), the subsequent residue calculation, and the square identity are correct.

However, the crucial assertion that **every** positive solution is
\[
u_n\sqrt2+v_n\sqrt3=(\sqrt2+\sqrt3)(5+2\sqrt6)^n
\]
is not proved. Multiplication by a unit generates a family of solutions, but does not by itself establish that the family is exhaustive; generalized Pell equations can have multiple orbits. Without an exhaustiveness argument, the later conclusion that the relevant index \(n\) is even does not apply to an arbitrary given solution.

This is a central logical gap, not a minor computational omission. The correct Pell reduction constitutes meaningful partial progress, but the submitted argument does not prove the result for all \(x,y\).
