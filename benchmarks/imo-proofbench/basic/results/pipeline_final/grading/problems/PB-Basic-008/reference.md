Renormalizing, we may suppose $a + b + c = 3$ and show $\sqrt{a}+\sqrt{b}+\sqrt{c} \geq ab+bc+ca$. Note that $\sqrt{a}+\sqrt{b}+\sqrt{c} \geq ab+bc+ca$ is equivalent to $2\sqrt{a}+2\sqrt{b}+2\sqrt{c} \geq 2ab+2bc+2ca \Leftrightarrow a^2+2\sqrt{a}+b^2+2\sqrt{b}+c^2+2\sqrt{c} \geq a^2+2ab+b^2+2bc+c^2+2ca = (a+b+c)^2$. The inequality is now equivalent to the inequality $a^2+2\sqrt{a}+b^2+2\sqrt{b}+c^2+2\sqrt{c} \geq 9$. Observe that from AM-GM, we have $a^2+\sqrt{a}+\sqrt{a} \geq 3a$. Similarly, we have $b^2+\sqrt{b}+\sqrt{b} \geq 3b$ and $c^2+\sqrt{c}+\sqrt{c} \geq 3c$. Adding these together, we have $a^2+2\sqrt{a}+b^2+2\sqrt{b}+c^2+2\sqrt{c} \geq 3(a+b+c) = 9$. Hence proved.
