\textbf{Claim.} $X,E,E_{2}$ are colinear.


 \noindent\emph{Proof. }Let $T$ be a intersection of $EE_{2}$ and
 $AC$. Observe that $CE_{1}=CE=CE_{2}$ and $(B,C,E,E_{1},E_{2})$
 are cyclic. This gives $\angle E_{2}E_{1}C=\frac{1}{2}(180^{\circ}-2\angle C)=\angle E_{2}TC$,
 so $(E_{2},T,C,E_{1})$ are cyclic. Thus, $T=X$, and we have $X,E,E_{2}$
 colinear.


 Now, observe $\angle OXC=90-\angle XE_{2}C=90-\angle EE_{2}C=90-\angle ABC=90-\angle ADE=90-\angle XDE$.
 Thus, $\angle OXC+\angle XDE=90^{\circ}$, and we have $XO\perp DE$
 as desired.
