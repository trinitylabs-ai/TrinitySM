I claim the answer is $136$. Take the construction $17, -1,...,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1$, which clearly works.
 Now I will show that this is the minimum. For any partition of $\{a_1, a_2, ..., a_{18}\}$ into six triplets, at least one triplet must satisfy that $a_i + a_j +a_k \ge 0$.
 Therefore there are $\frac{18!}{(3!)^6 6!}$ partitions, and a fixed triplet is contatined in $\frac{15!}{(3!)^5 5!}$ partitions, so there are at least $\frac{18!}{(3!)^6 6!}/\frac{15!}{(3!)^5 5!} = 136$ partitions satisfying the condition.
