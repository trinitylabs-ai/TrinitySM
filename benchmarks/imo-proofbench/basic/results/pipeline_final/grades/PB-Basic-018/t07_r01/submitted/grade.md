The algebraic transformations, recurrence congruences, and final square computation are correct. In particular, admissible solutions in the displayed Pell sequence have even index, and for such indices the expression indeed equals \(b_k^2\).

The only gap is the unproved assertion that **every** positive solution of
\[
3u^2-2v^2=1
\]
is generated from \((1,1)\) by multiplication by \(5+2\sqrt6\). This does not follow automatically merely from identifying the fundamental unit of the related Pell equation.

The gap is localized and readily repaired: for any solution with \(u>1\), one can show
\[
(5u-4v,\;5v-6u)
\]
is another positive solution with smaller first coordinate, giving descent to \((1,1)\). Thus the proposed parametrization is correct, but its exhaustiveness requires this missing justification.
