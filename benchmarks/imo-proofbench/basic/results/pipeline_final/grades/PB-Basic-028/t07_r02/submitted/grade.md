The coordinate formulas, tangency equation, similarity \(\triangle AEF\sim\triangle ABC\), and the final trigonometric verification are correct.

However, the final inference has a logical gap. Showing that
\[
z_0=\frac{2R\sin(B/2)\sin(C/2)\cos A}{\cos(A/2)}
\]
is a root of the quadratic does not prove that the actual \(z=AX\) equals \(z_0\), since the quadratic has another positive root. Indeed, by Vieta, the other root is
\[
\frac{2R^2\cos A\sin B\sin C}{z_0}
=4R\cos\frac A2\cos\frac B2\cos\frac C2=s,
\]
corresponding to the \(A\)-excircle configuration.

The solution must explicitly use the condition that \((W)\) is the circle closer to \(A\) to select \(z_0\), rather than the root \(s\). This is a genuine but readily repairable local omission; the core argument is otherwise complete.
