The final classification is correct, but the proof has a fatal gap in Step 2. From

w_n=Cn^r+O(1)
\]
one cannot conclude that \(\Delta^m w_n\to0\): a bounded sequence may have non-vanishing finite differences, as shown by \((-1)^n\). Consequently, the claims that \(w_n\) is eventually polynomial and that \(2k/d\) is an integer are not established.

Moreover, the assertion that a rational polynomial taking square values at all sufficiently large integers must itself be a polynomial square is a substantial lemma requiring proof. These are central steps, not minor omissions, so the solution is not eligible for 6 points.
