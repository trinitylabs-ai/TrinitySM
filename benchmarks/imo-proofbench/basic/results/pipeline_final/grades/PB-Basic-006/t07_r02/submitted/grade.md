The contradiction setup and deduction \(c_k\neq 0\) are correct. However, the central argument is invalid.

From Newton’s inequality
\[
S_j^2>S_{j-1}S_{j+1},
\]
one cannot conclude that \(S_{j-1}\) and \(S_{j+1}\) have the same sign: if their product is negative, the inequality holds automatically. Consequently, the asserted absolute log-concavity and decreasing ratio sequence do not follow.

For example,
\[
8-10x+x^2+x^3=(x-1)(x-2)(x+4)
\]
has three distinct real roots and nonzero coefficients, but its normalized coefficients are
\[
8,\quad -\frac{10}{3},\quad \frac13,\quad 1.
\]
Here
\[
\left|\frac13\right|^2<\left|-\frac{10}{3}\right|\cdot |1|,
\]
so the claimed absolute log-concavity fails. Thus the recurrence bounding \(|c_k|\), and hence the final contradiction, are unsupported.

The valid preliminary observations do not establish the key coefficient bound required by the partial-credit guideline.
