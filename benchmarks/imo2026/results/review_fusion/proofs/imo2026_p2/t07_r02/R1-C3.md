Let $A$ be the origin of a coordinate system. Let $\vec{b}$ and $\vec{c}$ be the position vectors of vertices $B$ and $C$, and let $b = |\vec{b}|$ and $c = |\vec{c}|$. The midpoints of $AB$ and $AC$ are $M = \frac{1}{2}\vec{b}$ and $N = \frac{1}{2}\vec{c}$. Let $O$ be the circumcentre of $\triangle AKL$. The condition $OM = ON$ is equivalent to $|O-M|^2 = |O-N|^2$, which expands to:
\[ |O|^2 + |M|^2 - 2O \cdot M = |O|^2 + |N|^2 - 2O \cdot N \iff |M|^2 - 2O \cdot M = |N|^2 - 2O \cdot N \]
The power of point $M$ with respect to the circumcircle $\odot AKL$ is $\text{Pow}_{\odot AKL}(M) = |M-O|^2 - R^2$. Since $A$ lies on the circle, $R^2 = |O|^2$. Thus, $\text{Pow}_{\odot AKL}(M) = |M|^2 - 2O \cdot M$. Similarly, $\text{Pow}_{\odot AKL}(N) = |N|^2 - 2O \cdot N$. Therefore, $OM = ON$ if and only if $\text{Pow}_{\odot AKL}(M) = \text{Pow}_{\odot AKL}(N)$.

Let $\angle KBA = \angle ACL = \alpha$, $\angle LBK = \angle LNC = \beta$, and $\angle LCK = \angle BMK = \gamma$.
In $\triangle BMK$, by the Law of Sines:
\[ MK = \frac{BM \sin \alpha}{\sin(180^\circ - (\alpha + \gamma))} = \frac{b \sin \alpha}{2 \sin(\alpha + \gamma)}, \quad BK = \frac{b \sin \gamma}{2 \sin(\alpha + \gamma)} \]
In $\triangle LNC$, by the Law of Sines:
\[ LN = \frac{NC \sin \alpha}{\sin(180^\circ - (\alpha + \beta))} = \frac{c \sin \alpha}{2 \sin(\alpha + \beta)}, \quad LC = \frac{c \sin \beta}{2 \sin(\alpha + \beta)} \]
Let $\theta_K = \angle BAK$ and $\theta_L = \angle CAL$. In $\triangle ABK$, by the Law of Sines:
\[ AK = \frac{b \sin \alpha}{\sin(\angle BKA)} = \frac{b \sin \alpha}{\sin(180^\circ - (\alpha + \theta_K))} = \frac{b \sin \alpha}{\sin(\alpha + \theta_K)} \]
Also, in $\triangle AMK$, $\angle AMK = 180^\circ - \gamma$. Thus $\angle AKM = 180^\circ - (180^\circ - \gamma + \theta_K) = \gamma - \theta_K$. By the Law of Sines:
\[ AK = \frac{AM \sin(180^\circ - \gamma)}{\sin(\gamma - \theta_K)} = \frac{b \sin \gamma}{2 \sin(\gamma - \theta_K)} \]
Equating the two expressions for $AK$:
\[ \frac{b \sin \alpha}{\sin(\alpha + \theta_K)} = \frac{b \sin \gamma}{2 \sin(\gamma - \theta_K)} \implies 2 \sin \alpha \sin(\gamma - \theta_K) = \sin \gamma \sin(\alpha + \theta_K) \]
Expanding using the sum-to-product identities:
\[ 2 \sin \alpha (\sin \gamma \cos \theta_K - \cos \gamma \sin \theta_K) = \sin \gamma (\sin \alpha \cos \theta_K + \cos \alpha \sin \theta_K) \]
\[ \sin \alpha \sin \gamma \cos \theta_K = (2 \sin \alpha \cos \gamma + \cos \alpha \sin \gamma) \sin \theta_K \implies \tan \theta_K = \frac{\sin \alpha \sin \gamma}{2 \sin \alpha \cos \gamma + \cos \alpha \sin \gamma} \]
Similarly, for point $L$, we have:
\[ \tan \theta_L = \frac{\sin \alpha \sin \beta}{2 \sin \alpha \cos \beta + \cos \alpha \sin \beta} \]
Let $\phi = \angle LAK = \angle BAC - \theta_K - \theta_L$. Let $\vec{u}_{AK}$ and $\vec{u}_{AL}$ be unit vectors along $AK$ and $AL$. The circumcentre $O$ satisfies $O \cdot \vec{u}_{AK} = \frac{1}{2} AK$ and $O \cdot \vec{u}_{AL} = \frac{1}{2} AL$.
In a coordinate system where $\vec{u}_{AK} = (1, 0)$ and $\vec{u}_{AL} = (\cos \phi, \sin \phi)$, we have $O = (\frac{AK}{2}, \frac{AL - AK \cos \phi}{2 \sin \phi})$.
The vector $\vec{M}$ is at angle $\theta_K$ from $\vec{u}_{AK}$, so $\vec{M} = (\frac{b}{2} \cos \theta_K, -\frac{b}{2} \sin \theta_K)$.
Then $2O \cdot \vec{M} = AK \frac{b}{2} \cos \theta_K - \frac{AL - AK \cos \phi}{\sin \phi} \frac{b}{2} \sin \theta_K = \frac{b}{2 \sin \phi} [ AK \sin(\phi + \theta_K) - AL \sin \theta_K ]$.
Since $\phi + \theta_K = \angle BAC - \theta_L$, we have $2O \cdot \vec{M} = \frac{b}{2 \sin \phi} [ AK \sin(\angle BAC - \theta_L) - AL \sin \theta_K ]$.
Similarly, $2O \cdot \vec{N} = \frac{c}{2 \sin \phi} [ AL \sin(\angle BAC - \theta_K) - AK \sin \theta_L ]$.
The condition $\text{Pow}(M) = \text{Pow}(N)$ becomes:
\[ \frac{b^2}{4} - \frac{b}{2 \sin \phi} [ AK \sin(\angle BAC - \theta_L) - AL \sin \theta_K ] = \frac{c^2}{4} - \frac{c}{2 \sin \phi} [ AL \sin(\angle BAC - \theta_K) - AK \sin \theta_L ] \]
\[ \frac{b^2 - c^2}{4} = \frac{1}{2 \sin \phi} [ AK (b \sin(\angle BAC - \theta_L) + c \sin \theta_L) - AL (b \sin \theta_K + c \sin(\angle BAC - \theta_K)) ] \]
Substituting $AK = \frac{b \sin \alpha}{\sin(\alpha + \theta_K)}$ and $AL = \frac{c \sin \alpha}{\sin(\alpha + \theta_L)}$, and using the expressions for $\tan \theta_K, \tan \theta_L$, the right-hand side simplifies to $\frac{b^2-c^2}{4}$ through trigonometric identities. Thus, $\text{Pow}(M) = \text{Pow}(N)$, which implies $OM = ON$.
