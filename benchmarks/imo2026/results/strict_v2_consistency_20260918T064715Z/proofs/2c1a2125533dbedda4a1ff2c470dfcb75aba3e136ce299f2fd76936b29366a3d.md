$$(c - b \cos A)x - (b \sin A)y = \frac{c^2 - b^2}{4}$$

$$\frac{BK}{\sin \phi_1} = \frac{c}{\sin(\phi_1 + \alpha)} \implies \frac{\sin \phi_1}{\sin(\phi_1 + \alpha)} = \frac{\sin \gamma}{2 \sin(\alpha + \gamma)}$$

$$\frac{CL}{\sin \phi_2} = \frac{b}{\sin(\phi_2 + \alpha)} \implies \frac{\sin \phi_2}{\sin(\phi_2 + \alpha)} = \frac{\sin \beta}{2 \sin(\alpha + \beta)}$$

$$2 [AK^2 (c h_L^{AB} - b h_L^{AC}) - AL^2 (c h_K^{AB} - b h_K^{AC})] = (c^2 - b^2) \cdot 2 \text{Area}(\triangle AKL)$$

$$AK (c \sin(A - \phi_2) - b \sin \phi_2) - AL (c \sin \phi_1 - b \sin(A - \phi_1)) = \frac{c^2 - b^2}{2} \sin(\phi_2 - \phi_1)$$
